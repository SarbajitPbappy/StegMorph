# Novelty round 7 (13 Nov 2026): author-supplied RIS exports and two new PDFs

## New full texts
- Luo, Huang, Huang 2010 (TIFS 5(2):201-214), read in full by text extraction. It proposes an edge-adaptive LSB-matching scheme and compares it with LSB, LSBM, LSBMR, PVD and IPVD schemes that embed at pseudo-random positions (and with HBC, AE-LSB), using RS analysis, two LSBM-specific detectors (Li-1D, Huang-1D) and four universal feature sets (Shi-78D, Farid-72D, Moulin-156D, Li-110D), on natural images. Consequence for our claims: the pattern "position-adaptive versus random-position embedding under trained feature detectors" is prior art from 2010; the schemes differ in operator and in modification rate (the paper itself notes AE-LSB has the lowest modification rate), so the position effect is not isolated, no CNN, no medical images, no subject-level splits, no heuristic/GA key. Our controlled operator-matched design with matched modification counts is still not in this paper. The article's Table 1 row and related-work text were corrected (previously 'not claimed here', abstract level).
- Li, Wang, Huang, Li 2014 (ICIP, HILL), read in full: high-pass KB residual, 3x3 and 15x15 low-pass averaging, tested on BOSSbase at 0.4 bpp with SRM and MCNUQ ensemble classifiers against HUGO, WOW, S-UNIWARD. Our implementation (Aletheia HILL.m: KB filter, 3x3 average, 15x15 average) matches the description.
